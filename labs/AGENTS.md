# 🧪 Labs: Agent Research & Experiments

This directory is reserved for **Agent-led research**, proof-of-concepts, and diagnostic experiments that fall outside the core production scope of **Luno Mission Control**.

## 📜 Policy
- **Transient Nature**: Content in this folder is considered temporary and subject to "Total Vaporization" protocols during public release cycles.
- **Isolation**: Experiments here must remain entirely decoupled from the production `Application` and `Domain` layers.
- **Documentation**: Significant findings should be migrated to the `/docs` directory or codified as ADRs (Architecture Decision Records) rather than living indefinitely in the lab.
